# The 9-Module Photography Brief Framework (`real-me-portrait`)

When translating a user's natural language request into a high-precision portrait prompt, select 1–2 parameters from each of the 9 modules below. Do not overload the prompt with contradictory style buzzwords.

---

## Module 1: Image Purpose (圖像用途)

| Purpose | Visual Priority (畫面重點) | Prompt Keywords (EN / ZH) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01. Social Media Avatar (社群頭像)** | Clear face, steady gaze, clean background | `clean social portrait, clear facial focus, uncluttered background` / `社群頭像人像，臉部清晰，眼神穩定，背景乾淨` |
| **02. Professional / Founder Portrait (職業/創業家寫真)** | Credible, natural, approachable authority | `professional founder portrait, credible and approachable` / `職業形象寫真，可信自然，專業有親和力` |
| **03. Magazine Editorial (雜誌風格人像)** | Framing, negative space, aura, mood | `editorial magazine portrait, intentional negative space, atmospheric` / `雜誌內頁感人像，講究構圖留白與整體氣質氛圍` |
| **04. Commercial Beauty (商業美妝特寫)** | Clean lighting, stable skin micro-texture, high control | `commercial beauty close-up, controlled studio lighting, luminous skin` / `商業美妝寫真，光線乾淨，膚質通透細膩` |
| **05. Lifestyle Portrait (生活方式人像)** | Relaxed, candid, captured in a real environment | `candid lifestyle portrait, relaxed everyday atmosphere` / `生活方式人像，自然鬆弛，像真實生活場景中抓拍` |
| **06. B&W Documentary (黑白紀實人像)** | Emotion, grain texture, human state, presence | `black-and-white documentary portrait, raw emotional presence` / `黑白紀實人像，強調情緒、紋理與現場感` |
| **07. Cinematic Night (夜景電影感人像)** | Light color contrast, shadow airiness, mood | `cinematic night portrait, atmospheric mixed lighting` / `夜景電影感人像，強調光色層次、暗部空氣感與故事氛圍` |

## Module 2: Subject, Aura & Makeup Anchor (主體、氣質與妝容錨點)

- **Temperament Options (氣質)**:
  - `natural and relaxed (自然鬆弛)` | `restrained and calm (克制冷靜)` | `sharp and professional (幹練專業)` | `warm and gentle (親和溫柔)` | `editorial aloofness (高級疏離)` | `playful and spirited (俏皮靈動)`
- **Expression Rhythm (表情狀態 — 避免單一商業假笑)**:
  - `calm gaze with a faint hint of a smile (平靜帶淡淡笑意)`
  - `eyes gently closed in a soft laugh (閉眼輕笑)`
  - `candid burst of laughter as if just amused (被逗笑後的自然笑意)`
  - `thoughtful and gentle looking slightly off-camera (若有所思但溫柔)`
- **Makeup Systems (妝容體系 — 整組保持統一)**:
  - **Clean Native (清透原生感)**: Minimal base, natural brow texture, hydrated matte lips, raw skin texture.
  - **Korean Editorial Studio (韓系雜誌清透妝)**: Translucent creamy skin, soft flat-arch brows, subtle peach-brown eyeshadow, defined natural aegyo-sal (臥蠶), fine eyelashes, soft rose/peach lip tint.

---

## Module 3: Lens Focal Length Language (鏡頭焦段語言)

| Focal Length | Perspective & Distance Feel (透視與距離感) | Recommended Prompt Phrase |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **24mm** | Strong spatial depth, dynamic environmental context | `24mm wide-angle environmental portrait look, dynamic spatial depth` |
| **35mm** | Street, documentary, storytelling closeness | `35mm lens look, candid storytelling distance, contextual environment` |
| **50mm** | Natural human-eye perspective, balanced lifestyle | `50mm standard lens look, natural perspective, balanced proportions` |
| **85mm** | **Gold standard for likeness**: stable facial proportions, clean separation | `85mm portrait lens look, natural facial proportions, clean background separation` |
| **105mm** | Beauty & commercial close-up, flattering compression | `105mm macro-portrait lens look, flattering facial compression` |
| **135mm** | Restrained, poetic distance, strong background compression | `135mm telephoto portrait look, strong background compression, quiet mood` |

---

## Module 4 & 5: Framing, Angle & Depth of Field (構圖、機位與景深)

- **Framing (構圖)**:
  - `face close-up (臉部特寫)` | `bust-up (胸像)` | `half-body (半身)` | `three-quarter body (3/4 身)` | `full-body (全身)` | `editorial cover composition with negative space (封面留白構圖)`
- **Camera Angle (機位)**:
  - `eye-level camera angle (平視機位 — 最穩臉型比例)`
  - `slightly high angle (略高機位 — 顯臉小且自然親切)`
  - `45-degree side angle (45度側面機位 — 強化輪廓立體感)`
- **Depth of Field (景深)**:
  - `f/1.8 feel` (Atmospheric night / strong bokeh)
  - `f/2 feel` (Lifestyle & editorial subject pop)
  - `f/2.8 feel, moderate shallow depth of field, background still recognizable` (**Safest daily default — 臉清楚、背景適度柔化不假**)
  - `f/4 feel` or `deep depth of field` (Preserves wardrobe, props, and street environment)

---

## Module 6: Lighting Setup (光線設計)

Always specify **source + softness + direction + eye catchlight**:
1. **Soft Window Light (柔和窗光)**: `soft natural window light from the left side, gentle shadow falloff, natural catchlights in the eyes`
2. **Large Diffused Studio Light (大面積棚拍柔光)**: `large diffused softbox lighting, even and creamy studio illumination, clean shadow transitions`
3. **Overcast Soft Light (陰天散射柔光)**: `overcast soft daylight, low-contrast urban illumination, stable skin tones`
4. **Golden Hour Backlight (黃金時刻逆光)**: `golden hour warm backlight, glowing hair rim light, soft natural lens flare, gentle front fill`
5. **Clamshell Beauty Lighting (美妝蚌殼光)**: `clamshell beauty lighting setup, crisp catchlights in the eyes, luminous shadowless facial lighting`
6. **Hard Direct Editorial Light (高對比硬光)**: `hard direct sunlight, crisp dramatic shadows, high-contrast fashion editorial mood`
7. **Neon & Tungsten Mixed Light (夜景混合光)**: `mixed neon and warm tungsten practical lights, atmospheric night illumination, realistic skin under mixed light`

---

## Module 7: Single Tone & Film Style Anchor (單一色調錨點 — 僅在使用者指定色調或底片感時套用)

> **Rule**: Default to `true-to-life natural color balance` unless the user asks for a specific mood, color palette, or film stock. Never force an unrequested film filter onto the image. When a tone anchor is needed, pick **ONLY ONE** per prompt:
- `true-to-life natural color balance, clean authentic skin tones` (**Default when no filter/film is specified by the user**)
- `neutral editorial tone` (Clean, true-to-life studio/founder magazine tone)
- `clean commercial tone` (High-clarity beauty & brand campaign tone)
- `Kodak Portra 400 inspired color tone` (Optional — only when warm lifestyle film look is requested)
- `Kodak Gold 200 inspired tone` (Optional — only when warm golden nostalgic vibe is requested)
- `Fujifilm Classic Chrome inspired muted palette` (Optional — only when low-saturation urban editorial tone is requested)
- `Fujifilm Eterna inspired cinema tone` (Optional — only when soft cinema narrative tone is requested)
- `CineStill 800T inspired night tone, subtle halation, restrained glow` (Optional — only when night neon/tungsten film look is requested)
- `Ilford HP5 inspired monochrome tone, subtle silver grain` (Optional — only when B&W documentary look is requested)

---

## Module 8 & 9: Realism Landmarks & Negative Constraints (真實感細節與負面限制)

- **Realism Landmarks**:
  `natural skin texture, subtle pores, realistic catchlights, focused gaze, natural flyaway hairs, real fabric texture, optical bokeh (not artificial blur), natural highlight roll-off, slight facial asymmetry, not over-retouched`
- **Core Negative Constraints**:
  `no AI fake face, no plastic skin, no over-retouched skin, no westernized facial features, no generic influencer look, no excessive bokeh, no unnatural catchlights, no distorted hands`
