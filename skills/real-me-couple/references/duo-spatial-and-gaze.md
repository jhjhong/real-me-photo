# Duo Spatial Choreography, Gaze Flow & Storyboard Templates (`real-me-couple`)

Use this reference to write bulletproof spatial coordinates, hand ownership rules, and multi-shot storyboards for two-person / couple photography.

---

## 1. Ready-to-Use Spatial & Limb Ownership Snippets (`雙人親密互動精確空間語法`)

Whenever generating close interaction, copy and adapt one of these verified spatial blocks:

### A. Shoulder Peek / Back Approach (`肩後探頭靠近`)
- **ZH**: `男生坐在前景偏左位置，女生坐在他後景偏右位置。女生從男生右肩後方自然探出臉看向鏡頭，右手輕輕搭在男生右肩上。男生低頭輕笑，目光看向畫面左下方。`
- **EN**: `The man sits in the foreground slightly left; the woman sits behind him slightly right, peeking her face over his right shoulder toward the camera with her right hand resting gently on his right shoulder, while he tilts his head down laughing softly toward the lower-left.`

### B. Chin Cupping / Face Touch (`溫柔托臉互動`)
- **ZH**: `女生在畫面右側，男生在畫面左側。男生用右手輕輕托住女生下巴，女生的左手自然搭在男生右手腕上。女生帶著淡淡笑意看向男生，男生目光專注溫柔地回應。`
- **EN**: `The woman is on the right side of the frame and the man is on the left. The man uses his right hand to gently cup the woman's chin, while her left hand rests naturally on his right wrist; their gazes meet with relaxed warmth.`

### C. Candid Back Hug Burst of Laughter (`背後環抱逗笑抓拍`)
- **ZH**: `女生從背後雙手環抱住男生脖子與肩膀，男生閉眼大笑，像剛被她逗到忍不住笑出來。女生也低頭笑，把左臉貼近男生右肩後側，動作像剛剛發生而非擺好再拍。`
- **EN**: `The woman wraps her arms around the man's shoulders from behind; the man closes his eyes in a genuine burst of laughter as if just amused by her, while she tilts her head down smiling with her cheek resting near his back shoulder, caught mid-moment.`

### D. Cheek-to-Cheek Close-Up (`近距離貼臉特寫 — 防手部變形版`)
- **ZH**: `兩人近距離貼臉半身或特寫，女生在左、男生在右，兩人的臉頰自然輕貼，頭身比例自然。雙手自然垂放於畫面下方或輕搭彼此肩側，不要讓複雜手指靠近臉部、眼睛或嘴巴，確保臉部與接觸線條乾淨真實。`
- **EN**: `Intimate cheek-to-cheek close-up with the woman on the left and the man on the right, cheeks gently touching with natural adult head proportions. Keep hands resting low on shoulders or out of frame—avoid complex fingers near eyes or lips.`

## 2. Modular Duo Shot Reference Pool (Dynamic Shuffle — Never Hardcode Fixed Shot Order)

> **Anti-Clone Rule**: When a user wants multiple couple shots or a grid without specifying every frame, **do NOT lock "Shot 1 must be frontal, Shot 2 must be a back hug, etc."** Treat the items below as a **dynamic, shuffleable pool**—randomly reorder and recombine them on every generation so repeated runs never produce identical layouts:

- **[Frontal Intimate Half-Body (`正面並肩貼近半身`)]**: Standing close side-by-side, shoulders gently touching, calm warm gaze into the lens or at each other.
- **[Candid Back-Hug Laugh (`背後環抱大笑抓拍`)]**: One hugs from behind; the other closes eyes laughing spontaneously.
- **[Quiet Embrace (`環抱情緒收斂瞬間`)]**: Same embrace settled into a quiet, gentle moment, looking off-frame with soft smiles.
- **[Partner A Focus + Partner B Hand Interaction (`人物 A 主視角 + 伴侶手部互動`)]**: Framing centers on one partner in a relaxed half-body shot while the other's hand enters cleanly from the side to gently cup their cheek or shoulder.
- **[Partner B Focus + Partner A Hand Interaction (`人物 B 主視角 + 伴侶手部互動`)]**: Framing centers on the other partner smiling softly while their partner's hand gently cups their chin or tucks hair.
- **[Cheek-to-Cheek Macro Close-Up (`近距離貼臉情緒特寫`)]**: Tight framing on both faces close together, eyes at different gaze angles, showcasing skin micro-texture and catchlights.
- **[Holding Hands / Detail Cutaway (`牽手/局部細節敘事特寫`)]**: Close-up of their hands naturally intertwined against fabric texture, adding editorial breathing room.
- **[Shoulder-to-Shoulder Seated (`並肩坐姿靠肩放鬆`)]**: Seated side-by-side, one resting their head lightly on the other's shoulder in a tranquil frame.

## 3. Couple Travel & Coastal Candid Snapshot Scenarios (`情侶海邊/旅行隨手拍真實感情境庫 — 預設以原圖版式及格式為主，不限定拼貼或比例`)

> **Layout, Aspect Ratio & Dynamic Shuffle Rule**:
> - By default, **follow the layout, format, and aspect ratio of the user's uploaded photo(s)**. **Do NOT force an unrequested `4×4` / `X×X` collage layout or alter the aspect ratio unless the user explicitly specifies it in their prompt.**
> - The scenarios below form an **un-ordered reference pool** (never hardcode "Panel 1 must be beach selfie"). Pick or shuffle them dynamically so every generation stays fresh:

- **Authentic Camera-Roll Optical Vibe (Can be used on any single shot or when requested)**:
  `authentic live-photo / candid camera-roll feel, warm natural sunlight highlights, subtle organic grain, natural windblown hair, slight candid motion vitality, sun-kissed real skin texture with subtle pores—not a stiff wedding studio portrait`.
- **High-Frequency Couple Travel Scenario Pool (Pick or dynamically shuffle; do not lock to fixed panel numbers)**:
  - **Sunny Beach Selfie (`海邊陽光貼臉自拍`)**: Bright coastal daylight, slightly warm highlights, squinting laugh in the sea breeze.
  - **Seaside Café Candid (`海邊露天咖啡館對坐視角`)**: Holding an iced drink outdoors, wind gently tousling hair, relaxed across-the-table gaze.
  - **Coastal Town Alley Walk (`海邊白牆小鎮漫步`)**: Walking through a sunlit alley in linen shirts, looking back mid-stride.
  - **Dim Hotel Mirror / Indoor Candid (`飯店房間鏡前日常隨手拍`)**: Warm indoor ambient lighting, casual mirror selfie together.
  - **Beach Look-Back (`沙灘回眸微笑`)**: Walking along the shoreline with ocean waves behind, turning back with a spontaneous smile.
  - **Close Cheek-to-Cheek Laugh (`近距離貼臉大笑特寫`)**: Tight framing on both faces laughing with eyes closed (hands kept low out of frame).
  - **Golden-Hour Sunset Overlook (`日落看海側影`)**: Leaning against a coastal railing at sunset, warm orange sky and glowing hair rim light.
  - **Pier / Lounge Chair Relaxation (`碼頭/躺椅鬆弛瞬間`)**: Seated casually by the water, effortless vacation mood.
  - **Night Street Hand-Holding Walk (`夜晚街頭牽手漫步`)**: Warm tungsten streetlamps, holding hands mid-walk with subtle cinematic motion.
  - **Poolside / Waterside Half-Body (`水畔/泳池邊清爽半身`)**: Clean daylight water reflections, natural sun-kissed skin texture.
  - **Windswept Playful Selfie (`迎風玩鬧自拍`)**: Hair flying in the coastal wind, dynamic unposed laughter.
  - **Partner Behind the Lens (`互拍紀錄視角`)**: One partner holding a camera/phone photographing the other in a candid travel moment.
  - **Burst-of-Laughter Extreme Close-Up (`開懷大笑超近景抓拍`)**: Spontaneous mid-laugh snapshot as if the shutter was pressed accidentally.
  - **Street Food / Gelato Date (`街邊分享美食/冰淇淋`)**: Casual street-side moment sharing local food or gelato (hands kept simple and natural).
  - **Night Balcony Breeze (`夜晚陽台/欄杆晚風回眸`)**: Soft city/harbor bokeh lights in the background, relaxed evening atmosphere.
  - **Cheek-Kiss Souvenir Shot (`親吻臉頰紀念照`)**: One kissing the other's cheek while the partner smiles with eyes gently closed.
- **Optional `4×4` Collage Mode (ONLY When Explicitly Requested by User)**:
  If and only if the user's prompt specifies a `4×4` collage (`16宮格/4×4相冊拼貼`), format as `4×4 collage (16 panels), thin black divider lines on a black background` and forbid complex fingers near faces across all 16 small panels.
